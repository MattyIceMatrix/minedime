What this proves, and what it doesn't

Prices here are simulated, so the answer key is known: the lab can check itself. Across 12 calibration markets, the tribunal approved real edges in 5 of 6 and approved nothing in 6 of 6 zero-edge markets. Real markets have weaker edges that decay, plus slippage, borrow costs and regime shifts this simulator does not model. Treat any result here as research, never as a signal to trade.
